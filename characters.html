Characters of the Inferno

Dante enters the underworld as a lost traveller. A guide, a ferryman and a judge shape the first stages of his journey.

Dante the Pilgrim

THE TRAVELLER

Dante loses his way in a dark wood. We see Hell through his eyes as he asks questions, listens to the dead and sometimes faints at what he hears.

Virgil

THE GUIDE

When Dante hesitates, Virgil tells him that Beatrice asked him to help. The Roman poet gives Dante the courage to follow him through the gates of Hell.

Charon

THE FERRYMAN

At the river Acheron, Charon gathers the dead into his boat. He spots Dante among the crowd and orders him away. A living man has come to a crossing meant for the dead.

Minos

THE JUDGE

At the entrance to the second circle, each soul confesses to Minos. He winds his tail around himself, and the number of coils tells the soul how many circles down it must go.
