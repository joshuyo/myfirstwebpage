The Nine Circles of Hell

According to Dante's Inferno, Hell has nine circles. Each circle has a different punishment depending on the sin committed.

01

Limbo

The home of virtuous people who lived without Christian baptism.

Punishment: Living without hope of seeing God.

02

Lust

People who allowed their desires to control their actions.

Punishment: Blown endlessly by powerful winds.

03

Gluttony

People who consumed food and drink excessively.

Punishment: Freezing, dirty rain and the three-headed Cerberus.

04

Greed

Those who hoarded wealth or wasted money carelessly.

Punishment: Pushing huge weights against each other.

05

Wrath

The angry and those who kept their anger inside.

Punishment: Fighting on or suffering beneath the River Styx.

06

Heresy

People who held beliefs Dante considered heretical.

Punishment: Imprisoned inside burning tombs.

07

Violence

This circle contains different kinds of violent sinners.

Punishment: Boiling blood, transformed trees, and burning sand.

08

Fraud

People who deliberately deceived and manipulated others.

Punishment: Different punishments across ten separate ditches.

09

Treachery

The lowest circle of Hell is reserved for traitors.

Punishment: Frozen in the lake Cocytus. Lucifer is found at the bottom.

Did You Know?

The ninth circle is divided into four areas based on different types of betrayal. The worst traitors are trapped deepest in the ice.
