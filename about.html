About Dante Alighieri

The poet behind The Divine Comedy.

Who Was Dante?

Dante Alighieri was an Italian poet born in Florence in 1265. He died in 1321.

He is best known for writing The Divine Comedy, one of the most important works of medieval literature.

The Divine Comedy

The poem describes Dante's imaginary journey through the afterlife.

It is divided into three main parts:

  1. Inferno - Hell
  2. Purgatorio - Purgatory
  3. Paradiso - Heaven

Why Is It Famous?

The Divine Comedy has influenced literature, art, movies, and video games for hundreds of years.

Dante's nine circles are part of his literary imagination and reflect medieval beliefs about morality.
